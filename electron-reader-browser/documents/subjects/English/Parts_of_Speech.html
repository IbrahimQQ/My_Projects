Parts of Speech

In English, words are classified into different categories called "parts of speech." Understanding these helps us build better sentences!

1. Nouns

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, or idea.
The dog ran across the park.
Sarah loves music.

2. Verbs

A verb is a word that shows action or a state of being.
She runs every morning.
The flowers are beautiful.

3. Adjectives

An adjective is a word that describes a noun.
The tall building has many windows.
She wore a beautiful dress.

4. Adverbs

An adverb is a word that describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb.
He runs quickly.
She sings beautifully.

5. Pronouns

A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun.
She went to the store.
They are my friends.

6. Prepositions

A preposition shows the relationship between a noun and other words.
The book is on the table.
She walked through the door.

7. Conjunctions

A conjunction connects words, phrases, or sentences.
I like apples and oranges.
She was tired but happy.

8. Interjections

An interjection expresses strong emotion.
Wow! That was amazing!
Ouch! That hurt!
Tip: Practice identifying parts of speech in sentences you read every day. The more you practice, the better you'll become at writing!
