The Solar System

Introduction

Our Solar System consists of the Sun and everything that orbits around it, including eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

The Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of our Solar System. It's a massive ball of hot gas that provides light and heat to all the planets.

Fun Fact: The Sun is so large that about 1.3 million Earths could fit inside it!

The Eight Planets

Inner Planets (Rocky Planets)

Mercury - The smallest planet and closest to the Sun

Venus - The hottest planet with thick clouds

Earth - Our home, the only planet with known life

Mars - The Red Planet with two moons

Outer Planets (Gas Giants)

Jupiter - The largest planet with the Great Red Spot

Saturn - Famous for its beautiful rings

Uranus - Rotates on its side

Neptune - The windiest planet
